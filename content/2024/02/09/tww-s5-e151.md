Restream - https://restream.io/

Stream a video signal to more than one platform at the same time

YouTube and Facebook Group for example

Other platforms that are available include X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, Twitch, Amazon Live

Downside is that it takes a short period to connect to the platforms and you lose the first few seconds

You can embed a stream on your website before going live using the studio option

Schedule Live Streams and also recorded videos

Stream feed can come from OBS - <a href="https://obsproject.com/">https://obsproject.com/</a>

Use different brands

Consolidate comments from different platforms

Studio option instead of OBS

There is a free plan available
