AI Moving forward

Appsumo - <a href="https://www.appsumo.com">https://www.appsumo.com</a>

Pismo - <a href="https://pismo.ai/">https://pismo.ai/</a>

Similar but different to Apple Intelligence which is built into the Apple product OS's

Pismo - Desktop app to select text in any application and perform AI functions with it

For example, translate, improve writing, fix spelling and grammar issues, shorten, summarise, set friendly or professional tone etc.

You can also add your own prompts, which is the big difference

IKI - <a href="https://iki.ai">https://iki.ai</a>

Upload pdf files and then query the contents using AI

Also allows transcription of YouTube videos (not working at the time of recording)

Use it for technical documents like manuals

AI is leaping forward, but there are serious potential issues that need to be looked at like generative AI art

Will report back in the future

Consistency

Struggled with consistency when comparing every day to the perfect day

This is not sustainable, as you can feel like you failed right at the beginning of day if something goes wrong

Things take longer than you think

Experience will help to know how long things take

Better to compare your day to an average day - it will normally be either slighter better or slightly worse

The gap between the two is much smaller

Getting into the right frame of mind if things are going well
